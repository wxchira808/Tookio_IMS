## Auditability
I. Role-based access control aligned to
KenTrade’s organizational structure
and information classification policy.

2019 (Kenya) and other applicable
privacy frameworks.

## Audit Planning
The system shall enable one to:
i. Create annual or ad-hoc audit plans
and revision of the same with
adequate authorization and
supporting documentation upload.
ii. Create audit programs & planning
memos
iii. Schedule audits with recurring or one-
time frequency.
iv. Schedule audits as per risk-based plan.
v. Assign auditors and auditees to audits.
vi. Allocate resources such as financial to
specific audits.
vii. Configure auto-notifications and
reminders for scheduled audits.

Risk Assessment and Evaluation
i. Customizable risk scoring models
incorporating likelihood, impact,
velocity, and control effectiveness.

Preventive Actions (CAPA) progress.
iv. Configurable workflows for risk

decisions.

and business continuity systems to ensure
end-to-end control linkage.

Non-Functional Requirements
System Performance and Scalability
The system shall: -
i. Support concurrent access by at least 200
users without degradation in
performance.
ii. Response time for core operations (risk
search, updates, dashboard loading)
shall not exceed three seconds under
normal load.
iii. The platform shall be scalable both
vertically and horizontally, enabling
addition of users, data, and AI models
without system re-engineering.
iv. Capable of handling large data volumes
from multiple sources (e.g., audit data,
incident feeds, risk indicators) with real-
time or near-real-time analytics.
v. Built-in auto-scaling and load-balancing
mechanisms for cloud or hybrid
deployments to maintain optimal system
availability.

Availability, Reliability, and Business
## Continuity
i. System uptime shall be a minimum of
99.9%, excluding scheduled
maintenance.
ii. Must include redundant infrastructure
and support deployment in high-

(DR) environments.
iii. Automated data backup and recovery

for fault tolerance and graceful
degradation under stress.
vi. Ability to support an increasing
number of users and audit records over
time.
vii. Modular expansion for future
functionality.

Security and Data Protection
i. Compliance with the Kenya Data
Protection Act, 2019, and ISO 27001
information security standards.
ii. Implementation of role-based access
control (RBAC) with least-privilege
principles.
iii. Integration with Active Directory (AD)
and Multi-Factor Authentication
(MFA) for user authentication.
iv. End-to-end encryption of data in
transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest
## (AES-256).
v. Periodic security patching,
vulnerability scans, and penetration
testing.
vi. Complete audit trail of all user actions,
configuration changes, and access
attempts.
vii. Secure logging and monitoring
compatible with KenTrade’s SIEM or
cybersecurity monitoring tools.
viii. Automatic session timeout, IP
whitelisting, and CAPTCHA
verification for enhanced protection.

Data Quality and Integrity
The system shall provide for:-
i. Built-in validation rules to ensure
accuracy, completeness, and consistency
of data entered or imported.

detection mechanisms.

identifying inconsistencies or outliers in

System to ensure:
i. All AI/ML algorithms must be
explainable, ensuring decisions and
recommendations are transparent and
auditable.
ii. System shall include bias detection
and mitigation mechanisms in AI
models.
iii. Data used for AI training must
comply with privacy and ethical
standards, and model retraining must
be logged.
iv. Capability for human-in-the-loop
review before applying AI-driven
recommendations in critical processes.
