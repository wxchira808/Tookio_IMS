# TOOKIO - COMPANY OVERVIEW & SERVICES

## 🏢 **WHAT TOOKIO DOES**

**Tookio** is a Kenyan technology company specializing in **business management systems** that help organizations streamline operations, improve efficiency, and ensure compliance.

---

## 🎯 **OUR CORE SERVICES**

### **1. ERPNext Implementation & Customization**
We help businesses implement and customize **ERPNext** - a comprehensive business management platform that handles:
- **Financial Management** (Accounting, Invoicing, Payments)
- **Sales & CRM** (Customer management, Lead tracking, Sales pipeline)
- **Inventory Management** (Stock control, Warehouse management, Procurement)
- **Human Resources** (Employee records, Payroll, Leave management)
- **Manufacturing** (Production planning, Bill of materials, Quality control)
- **Project Management** (Task tracking, Time sheets, Resource allocation)

### **2. Incident Management Systems (IMS)**
We've developed a comprehensive **Incident Management System** specifically for Kenyan enterprises that includes:
- **Complete incident lifecycle tracking** (from report to resolution)
- **Investigation workflows** with evidence management and witness interviews
- **Risk assessment** with impact analysis and regulatory compliance
- **Real-time dashboards** and executive reporting
- **Automated notifications** and SLA monitoring
- **Audit trails** for regulatory requirements

### **3. Custom Management Systems Design**
We design and build **custom business management solutions** tailored to specific industry needs:
- **Workflow automation** for business processes
- **Document management** and approval systems  
- **Compliance tracking** and regulatory reporting
- **Performance dashboards** and analytics
- **Integration** with existing business systems
- **Mobile-responsive** web applications
